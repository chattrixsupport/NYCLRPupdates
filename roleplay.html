Ways to Roleplay

Create a character, build a scene, and make the story enjoyable for everyone involved.

01

Make a character

Think about who your character is, what they do, and what they want. Keep your actions consistent with their role.

02

Start a scene

Use what is happening around you to begin a believable interaction, such as a traffic stop, a call for help, or an everyday moment in the city.

03

Keep it realistic

Match your choices to the situation. Explain what your character does, then give others room to react—don’t decide their actions for them.

04

Build the story together

Listen to the other players, communicate clearly, and treat everyone respectfully. The best scenes give everyone a chance to take part.
